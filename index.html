About SupplyOps

Built for the people who actually open the supply closet.

SupplyOps started as a tool for one small business that kept running out of things at the worst possible moment. Inventory lived on a clipboard, in a group text, and in somebody's head — and none of those agreed with each other. Every ordering decision was a guess.

The software built to fix that turned out to be useful to a lot of other people, so it became a product. It's still shaped by the same test it started with: would a crew actually use this at 6am without being trained on it?

Up and running in a few minutes

Create your account

Email and a password. No sales call, no demo to schedule, no card until your trial ends.

Add what you stock

Name, how many you keep on hand, how many you have now. Scan a barcode onto it if you want. That's the whole setup.

Let your crew log usage

Park a tablet by the closet in Kiosk mode, or scan items with a barcode gun. Counts update as people take things.
